## Verified email activation

### Customer signup
A customer must provide a syntactically valid administrator email address. SecureFile sends an email-verification link to that address. The Company Admin cannot log in until the address is verified.

### Company Admin invitations
When a Company Admin creates an Employee or Client, the API validates the email address before creating the invitation. SecureFile sends the invitation to that address. The invited user must open the invitation and set a password; accepting the invitation marks the email as verified and activates the account.

This means user accounts are not activated from an unverified email address.
