# SecureFile Plan Pricing Update

Customer-facing package pricing:
- Basic: $10/month, 1 user, 5 GB included.
- Advanced: $95/month, 5 users, 2 GB included.
- Premium: $135/month, 10 users, 2 GB included.
- Enterprise: custom pricing; existing custom formula remains $10 first user + $5/additional user + $0.30/GB + add-ons.

Internal plan codes remain STARTER/BUSINESS/PROFESSIONAL/CUSTOM for database compatibility.
Advanced and Premium fixed plan prices are not recalculated from the custom usage formula; their published package prices are $95 and $135 respectively.
