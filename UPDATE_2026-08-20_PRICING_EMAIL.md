# SecureFile — Pricing & Verified Email Update

## Starter plan

Starter is now exactly **$10/month**:

- 1 Company Admin user
- 5 GB included storage at no extra storage charge
- No paid add-ons included

Business and Professional retain their existing package configuration and pricing. Custom pricing remains configurable using the existing $10 first-user + $5 additional-user + $0.30/GB + add-on pricing model.

## Customer signup email

The customer must provide a valid administrator email address. SecureFile sends an email verification link and the Company Admin cannot log in until the email is verified.

## Company Admin user invitations

When a Company Admin creates an Employee or Client, the API validates the email address. The account is initially INVITED and the invitation is sent to that email. The invited person must accept the invitation and create a password. Accepting the invitation marks the email verified and activates the account.

This prevents an unverified/fake email from becoming an active user account.
